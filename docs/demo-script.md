# NetLinks Demo Script v3: Managed IT Support

**Length:** 10:00 live demo, with a 6-minute cut-down version in section 4
**Category:** Managed IT support & end-user computing
**AI mode:** Cached by default. Live Bedrock is available behind an admin toggle.
**Auth:** Simulated sign-in (persona picker) and a "Switch account (demo)" menu. Production uses Cognito.
**Data:** All fictional. Every screen shows a "Fictional demo data" banner.

## 1. Demo data

### The opportunity
**Buyer:** Ubuntu Office Parks (fictional)
**Title:** "Managed IT support & end-user computing: 450 users, 3 sites (Midrand, Centurion, Sandton), 36-month contract, 4-hour on-site response SLA"

| Type | Requirement | How it's checked |
|---|---|---|
| Mandatory | Valid **SARS tax compliance status** | Rule: expiry date > closing date |
| Mandatory | **CSD registration** number | Rule: present + format |
| Mandatory | ≥51% Black-owned **or** B-BBEE Level 1–2 | Rule |
| Mandatory | Operates in **Gauteng** | Rule: service regions include GP |
| Mandatory | **Professional indemnity insurance ≥ R2m**, current | Rule: amount ≥ 2,000,000 and expiry > closing date |
| Mandatory | **Information security & POPIA policy** provided | Rule: document present, classified as policy |
| Mandatory | ≥ **2 technicians with current certifications** (e.g. CompTIA A+, Microsoft 365 Certified, ITIL 4 Foundation) | Rule: count of certs with readable, unexpired dates ≥ 2 |
| Preferred (40%) | Service desk, desktop/device support, Microsoft 365 administration | Semantic + structured |
| Preferred (25%) | 3+ years' experience; comparable clients of 200+ users | Structured |
| Preferred (20%) | ≥ **8 technicians**; service desk hours cover 07:00–18:00 | Structured |
| Preferred (15%) | On-site response feasible within 4 hours (base within ~50 km of Midrand) | Structured |

Why IT works well here: it plays to your team's own background, the POPIA policy rule ties straight into the platform's data-protection story (an IT provider will touch the buyer's personal data), and IT services get described in many different ways, which makes semantic matching easy to show.

## 2. Script (10:00)

### 0:00–0:40 · The problem
> "Youth-owned IT companies can do the work, but procurement teams can't quickly see it. A buyer sourcing IT support checks tax, insurance, certifications, security policies and technician capacity across dozens of PDFs, and 'IT support' gets described a hundred different ways. NetLinks turns those documents and the buyer's requirements into a structured, evidenced, explainable shortlist, and a human still makes every decision."

### 0:40–1:15 · Landing, sign-in, three users
- Open the **landing page** (~15 s): the headline, how it works, the manifesto.
- Click **Sign in to the demo**.
- Choose **SME → Thuto Tech Solutions** (demo email thuto@demo.netlinks.local) and open the SME workspace. No password is needed.
- Say: *"Sign-in is simulated for the demo. In production this is Amazon Cognito login, and the API enforces role permissions either way. Later role changes use **Switch account (demo)** in the top bar."*
- Point at the **"AI mode: Cached"** indicator: *"Extraction results were generated by our pipeline from these exact documents and cached, so the demo doesn't depend on venue Wi-Fi."*

*(Timing: the problem statement is trimmed by 5 s to make room for the landing moment; total still 10:00.)*

### 1:15–3:15 · SME: upload → extract → confirm (Thuto)
1. **Switch account (demo): SME (Thuto).** Upload `Thuto_Capability_Statement.pdf`, `Thuto_Technician_Certifications.pdf` and `Thuto_InfoSec_POPIA_Policy.pdf`.
2. The extracted profile appears: services, regions, technician count, certifications (name, holder, expiry), years trading, past clients + user counts, service desk hours, PI insurance amount + expiry, tax expiry.
3. **Lineage badges:** every field shows `AI-extracted` and a **"view source"** link that highlights the sentence in the document.
4. **Low-confidence flag:** PI cover written as "R2 mil professional liability". Thuto confirms it as R2,000,000.
5. **Correction:** the doc says 9 technicians; Thuto updates it to 11 (two new hires). Badge becomes `SME-confirmed`, and the change is logged.
6. **Consent prompt:** "Share this profile with buyers on NetLinks?" Thuto accepts.

> "AI suggests. The SME confirms. Nothing is treated as verified just because a model read it, and the SME controls who sees their profile."

### 3:15–4:30 · Buyer: the opportunity (Naledi)
1. **Switch account (demo): Buyer.** Paste the raw RFQ text and click **Structure requirements** (cached AI result).
2. NetLinks proposes Mandatory vs Preferred criteria. Naledi **edits one**: raises the PI insurance minimum from R1m to R2m.
3. Show the fit weights (40 / 25 / 20 / 15) as **visible, editable settings**.

> "The AI helps structure the RFQ, but mandatory rules run as plain code. Same input, same answer, every time."

### 4:30–6:45 · Matching & explainable shortlist
Click **Run matching**. Results show in three groups: **Eligible (2) · Needs Review (2) · Not Eligible (1)**.

1. **Thuto (#1):** score breakdown per component, each with an evidence snippet + source link. All 7 mandatory checks are green, each linked to its document (including the 3 current certifications).
2. **Mmapula (#2):**
   *"'Workplace technology care: device lifecycle, Microsoft 365 tenant administration, remote and on-site user assistance' matched to 'service desk, desktop support, M365 administration'. AI-assisted semantic match."*
   Capacity: 6 technicians vs 8 preferred, so lower score. Explained in plain language.
3. **Zola (Needs Review):** *"Professional indemnity cover expires 10 days before the closing date."* Show the dates side by side. **Not rejected.**
4. **Bright Path (Needs Review):** *"Technician count conflict: profile 15, capability statement 4."* Both sources side by side.
5. **Lerato (Not Eligible):** *"No Gauteng service region (confirmed: Western Cape only)."*

> "A keyword search would have missed Mmapula and could have rejected Zola outright. Missing, outdated or conflicting information goes to a human. Only confirmed facts can fail a mandatory rule."

### 6:45–7:45 · Buyer decision & audit trail
1. Naledi **shortlists** Thuto and Mmapula.
2. Zola: **Request information**, "Please upload your renewed professional indemnity schedule." Status becomes *Awaiting SME*.
3. Bright Path: **Request clarification** on technician count.
4. **Override:** Naledi moves Mmapula to the top of her shortlist with a written reason ("strongest Microsoft 365 references from comparable clients"). The override is recorded with the reason.
5. Open the **audit log**: extraction → SME corrections → consent → requirement edit → match run → requests → override + reason. All timestamped and attributed to a role. (newest first; use the Event type filter if needed)

### 7:45–8:30 · SME readiness view
- **Switch account (demo): SME (Zola):** sees the buyer's request plus the action *"Upload a renewed PI insurance schedule covering the contract start date."* Uploads the renewal, re-run, now **Eligible**. *(This closes the loop live.)*
- **Switch account (demo): SME (Lerato):** *"This opportunity requires Gauteng operations."* Readiness actions are shown; they don't change the current ranking.

> "Readiness guidance helps SMEs qualify next time. It never quietly changes the current ranking."

### 8:30–9:15 · YES dashboard
- **Switch account (demo): YES Admin.**
- Pipeline: eligible / needs review / not eligible, before and after Zola's fix
- **Top readiness gaps:** expiring insurance, conflicting capacity data, regional coverage
- **Frequently requested skills:** Microsoft 365, service desk, device management *(the brief asks for this insight)*
- Buyer decisions + overrides (count, with reasons)
- Fairness view: outcomes by province and business size *(small sample; framed as the mechanism, not a finding)*

> "At scale, this tells YES where to aim support. For example: 'IT SMEs are losing out on expired insurance and missing certifications, not capability.'"

### 9:15–10:00 · How it's built & close
- One architecture slide: Next.js PWA → FastAPI (ECS Fargate, Cape Town) → PostgreSQL + pgvector, S3 (private, encrypted) → Bedrock for extraction & semantic matching.
- Responsible AI in one line: *rules for eligibility, AI for understanding, humans for decisions, audit log for everything.*

> "You've seen one complete, working journey. Next: funding and grant matching, structured buyer feedback with an SME right of reply, and portal integrations, validated in an 8–12 week pilot with one buyer and 25–50 SMEs."

## 5. Likely judge questions

| Question | Short answer |
|---|---|
| "Is the AI actually running, or is this canned?" | Cached results came from our pipeline on these exact files. Switch to Live and we'll run it now. |
| "What if the AI extracts something wrong?" | It's labelled AI-extracted, linked to source, confirmed by the SME, and can't fail a mandatory rule on its own. Unreadable or outdated values go to review. |
| "Do you verify certifications?" | Not in the prototype. We show SME-confirmed vs verified separately. Checking against vendor certification records is a pilot item, subject to access. |
| "Isn't this just keyword search?" | Mmapula never uses the buyer's terms and still matches. Semantic matching feeds only the capability score (40%); everything else is structured data. |
| "Couldn't SMEs inflate their numbers?" | Conflicts between sources are flagged (Bright Path). SME-confirmed ≠ verified, and the buyer sees the difference. |
| "How is this fair?" | Only opportunity-relevant criteria are scored, incomplete records go to review, and outcomes are monitored by province and size. |
| "Where does the data live?" | Core data and documents in AWS Cape Town. AI inference is a documented dependency; we confirm the inference region and POPIA position before live pilots. |
| "Why is login simulated?" | Demo simplification. Role permissions are enforced server-side in the API; Amazon Cognito is planned for production. |
| "Why would a corporate pay?" | Less screening time and an auditable shortlist trail. The pilot measures both against a baseline. |
